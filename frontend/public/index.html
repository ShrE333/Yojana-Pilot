A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Yojana-Pilot is an independent assistance project. Here’s what it can — and can’t — help with.

Is Yojana-Pilot an official government service?

No. Yojana-Pilot is an independent project, not a government portal or an approved government partner. It provides informational guidance and links to official sources.

Does a recommendation guarantee that I’m eligible?

No. Recommendations reflect the facts you share and the catalog rules available. Missing facts, unreviewed information or additional requirements may change the outcome. Confirm current rules on official government websites.

Which schemes and languages are supported?

You can write in English, Hindi or Marathi. Coverage depends on the existing catalog, including central and Maharashtra schemes; it is not a complete directory of every scheme in India. Language understanding requires a configured model service.

Can Rescue Mode check my payment or submit an appeal?

No live payment or beneficiary records are queried, and no appeals are submitted. For configured schemes, Rescue Mode interprets the portal text you provide and suggests checks and corrective actions.

Do I need to upload identity documents?

No. Do not upload documents or enter identifying account numbers. Optional Rescue name comparisons use only the names you type. Share only what you are comfortable sending to this service.

Can I use WhatsApp or call the assistant?

WhatsApp text assistance requires an administrator-configured WAHA deployment and explicit opt-in. Configuration does not guarantee connectivity. LiveKit calls and voice notes are planned and do not work yet.
